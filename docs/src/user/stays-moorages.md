# Stays & moorages

A stay is the time you spend in one place between two trips. A moorage is the place itself. To learn how PostgSail detects them, see [How it works](how-it-works.md).

## Stays

Open **Stays** in the menu to see where you stayed, when you arrived and left, and for how long. You can filter by name, date range or stay type.

Open a stay to see its moorage and duration, change its stay type, or add notes.

## Moorages

Open **Moorages** in the menu to see every place you've stayed, with:

- its default stay type;
- the total time you spent there, in days;
- the number of arrivals and departures.

Open the **Map of Moorages** to see them all on a map, with the trips in and out of each one.

### A moorage's page

Open a moorage to:

- **Rename it** with **Preferred Name**. Every log that starts or ends there is updated.
- **Set its Default Stay Type.** Every stay at this moorage is updated, and future visits use it too.
- **Mark it as Home.** Your home ports count separately in your [statistics](stats.md), for example time at home versus time away.
- **Add notes.**
- **See its history:** number of visits, total time there, arrivals and departures, and when you were first and last there.

### Delete a moorage

**Delete moorage** also deletes every stay and every log that starts or ends there. This can't be undone. To fix a wrong name or stay type, edit the moorage instead.

## Stay types

PostgSail has four built-in stay types: Unknown, Anchor, Mooring Buoy and Dock. You can't change those, but you can add your own, like Marina, Yacht Club or Raft-up:

1. Open your **Profile** from the user menu and select the **Stay Type** tab.
2. Under **Add New Stay Type**, enter a name.
3. Optionally, choose what it **Falls back to**. If you ever delete your type, badges and statistics roll up to that type instead.

You can rename or delete your own types at any time.
