# Grafana dashboards

PostgSail comes with [Grafana](https://grafana.com) dashboards for your boat's data, at [app.openplotter.cloud](https://app.openplotter.cloud/). You can use the ready-made dashboards, and create and manage your own.

## Before you start

- **Create an account** on [auth.openplotter.cloud](https://auth.openplotter.cloud). Grafana and the other services use this account.
- **Log at least one trip.** Grafana opens once your boat has a log. PostgSail notifies you when your monitoring application is ready.

## Open Grafana

Select **Grafana** in the menu. It opens in a new window.

If your boat has no log yet, the page tells you there's no data yet. Go sailing first.
