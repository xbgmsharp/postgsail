# Statistics

Open **Stats** in the menu to see your sailing at a glance. Pick a date range: **All time**, **This month**, **Last month** or **Last 12 months**.

| Section      | What you'll find                                                                                            |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Logs         | Number of logs, first and last entry, longest sail, max speed, max wind speed, total distance, time underway |
| Moorages     | Home ports, unique moorages, countries visited, time at home ports, time spent away                          |
| Top logs     | Your best logs by distance, duration, average speed, max speed and max wind speed                            |
| Top moorages | Your moorages ranked by stay duration and by number of arrivals                                              |
| Badges       | The [badges](badges.md) you've earned                                                                        |

Home port figures rely on the moorages you mark as **Home**. See [Stays & moorages](stays-moorages.md).

## Share your statistics

Make your statistics public in your Profile. They're then available at `https://iot.openplotter.cloud/<your boat>/stats`. See [Profile, sharing & privacy](settings.md#sharing).
