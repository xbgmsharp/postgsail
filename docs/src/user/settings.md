# Profile, sharing & privacy

Your **Profile**, in the user menu, holds your settings. It has six tabs.

| Tab           | What it's for                                                           |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| General       | Your details, display preferences and account                           |
| Sharing       | What others can see                                                     |
| Stay Type     | Your own stay types. See [Stays & moorages](stays-moorages.md#stay-types). |
| Notifications | Email, Pushover and Telegram. See [Notifications](notifications.md).    |
| Alerting      | Alert thresholds. See [Notifications](notifications.md#alerts).         |
| Integrations  | [NoForeignland](noforeignland.md) and [AI agents (MCP)](../mcp.md)       |

## General

- **Name, Website and Instagram Handle:** shown alongside your statistics.
- **Language:** English, French, Spanish, German or Portuguese.
- **Theme:** Light, Dark, or Auto to follow your system.
- **Preferred Homepage:** the page you see after you log in.
- **Use Imperial Units:** show °F, feet and knots instead of °C, meters and km/h.

## Sharing

Your data is private by default. To share it, turn on **Public Profile**, choose the name your boat appears under, then pick what to share:

| Option                | Public address                                       |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Log Details           | `https://iot.openplotter.cloud/<your boat>/log/<id>` |
| Logs List             | `https://iot.openplotter.cloud/<your boat>/logs`     |
| Stats                 | `https://iot.openplotter.cloud/<your boat>/stats`    |
| Timelapse             | `https://iot.openplotter.cloud/<your boat>/timelapse` |
| Tracking & Monitoring | `https://iot.openplotter.cloud/<your boat>/monitoring` |

Public pages are read-only, and anyone can open them without logging in. To restrict them, turn on **Password Protected**: visitors then need your password to see your public data.

A public profile also lets you:

- publish your boat as a [Windy weather station](windy.md);
- let other PostgSail users find and follow your boat. See [Friends](friends.md).

## Privacy

PostgSail is hosted in a European sovereign cloud and complies with GDPR. You own your data:

- **Export** any log from its page, as GPX, GeoJSON, KML or an image. See [Logbook](logbook.md).
- **Delete your account** from the **General** tab. This permanently deletes your account and all your data, and can't be undone.

The full privacy policy is on the **Privacy** page of the app.
