# NoForeignland

[noforeignland](https://www.noforeignland.com) is a free community platform built by sailors for sailors. It has destination information added by cruisers, and a map where you can follow your friends' boats.

PostgSail can publish your boat to the noforeignland community map. Your boat then shows up there next to your friends, with no extra tracker to set up.

## Requirements

A noforeignland account with your boat set up.

## Turn it on

1. On [noforeignland.com](https://www.noforeignland.com), go to **Boat settings → Boat tracking** and copy your **API Key**.
2. In PostgSail, open your **Profile** from the user menu and select the **Integrations** tab.
3. Paste the key into **Boat API Key**.
4. Turn on **Publish to NoForeignland**.

To stop publishing, turn **Publish to NoForeignland** off.
