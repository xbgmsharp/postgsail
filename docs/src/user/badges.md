# Badges

PostgSail awards badges as you sail. Open **Badges** in the menu to see the ones you've earned, the ones still locked, and your progress in each category. An earned badge shows the date and the log that earned it.

| Badge             | How to earn it                                                           |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Helmsman          | Log your first sail                                                      |
| Navigator Award   | Sail more than 100 NM in one go                                          |
| Captain Award     | Sail more than 1,000 NM in total                                         |
| Aloha Award       | Sail more than 2,300 NM across the Pacific                               |
| Speed Demon       | Sail 100 NM or more in 24 hours. Updated each time you beat your record. |
| Wake Maker        | Reach 15 knots or more                                                   |
| Stormtrooper      | Sail in winds above 30 knots                                             |
| Explorer          | Spend 10 days away from your home port                                   |
| Mooring Pro       | Spend 10 nights on a mooring buoy                                        |
| Anchormaster      | Spend 25 days at anchor                                                  |
| Traveler          | Sail to another country                                                  |
| Tropical Traveler | Sail in tropical latitudes                                               |
| Club Alaska       | Sail in Alaska                                                           |
| Proud Owner       | Show off your boat                                                       |
| Social Beacon     | Make your logs public                                                    |
| Fleet Commander   | Register more than one boat                                              |
| AI Co-Pilot       | Turn on the AI agents (MCP) integration                                  |
| Sponsor           | Sponsor PostgSail                                                        |

If you delete one of your own stay types, badges like Mooring Pro and Anchormaster count those stays under the type it falls back to. See [Stays & moorages](stays-moorages.md#stay-types).

Setting a new 24-hour distance record also sends you a notification. Your earned badges appear on your [statistics](stats.md) too.
