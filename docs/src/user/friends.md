# Friends

Follow your sailing friends' boats and see them on a map, whether they use PostgSail or not.

## Follow a boat

1. Open **Friends** in the menu and click **Follow a boat**.
2. Enter one or more MMSI numbers, separated by commas.
3. Click **Follow**.

Boats that don't use PostgSail can still be followed if they have an AIS transponder: PostgSail tracks them through its AIS network.

Your friends then appear on the **Map** tab, and in the **List** tab with their status, last position and distance from you.

## Get alerts about a friend

Click a friend's boat to:

- give it a **Label** you'll recognize;
- turn on **Notify me on arrival** and **Notify me on departure**;
- get notified when it comes close, with **Notify me when within (nm)**. Set it to 0 to turn proximity alerts off;
- **Unfollow** it.

Alerts reach you through your usual channels. See [Notifications](notifications.md).

## Your followers

The **Followers** list shows who follows your boat. If your profile is private, other users can't find and follow you. To change that, see [Profile, sharing & privacy](settings.md#sharing).
