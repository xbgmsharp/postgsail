# Logbook

Every trip PostgSail detects becomes a log. To learn when a log starts and ends, see [How it works](how-it-works.md).

A new log appears once your boat has sent the data from the end of the trip. Keep your boat powered and online for a little while after you arrive.

## The log list

Open **Logs** in the menu to see all your trips, with where and when each one started and ended. You can filter by name, date range or tags.

From the list, you can:

- **Edit** a log;
- **Replay** it as a [timelapse](timelapse.md);
- **Merge** two logs (see below);
- **Delete** a log.

## A log's page

Click a log to open it. You'll find:

- **The track** on a map;
- **Departed and Arrived:** the moorages and times;
- **Duration and Distance;**
- **Speed and Wind:** average and maximum;
- **Sailing and Motoring:** time under sail and under engine;
- **Fuel and tanks:** fuel consumed and tank levels, when your boat sends that data;
- **Trip Metrics:** every data point recorded during the trip, as a table.

## Notes, tags and observations

Make each log your own:

- **Notes:** tell what happened.
- **Tags:** group logs, for example by season or crew, then filter the list by tag.
- **Observations:** record the sea state, cloud coverage and visibility.
- **Photos:** add pictures from the trip, as JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB each.

## Journal

Open **Journal** in the menu for a logbook of a whole period, ready to print. Pick a date range to see the days, trips and stays, with each trip's figures, observations and notes.

## Export, share and print

- **Export** a log as GPX, GeoJSON, KML or a PNG image of the track.
- **Share** a log with a link. Others can open it only if you make your log details public: see [Profile, sharing & privacy](settings.md).
- **Print** a log for your paper logbook.

## Merge two logs

Sometimes one trip gets split in two, for example after a short stop. To merge them, select **Merge** in the log list:

- the start log must be earlier than the end log;
- the two logs must be next to each other, with no other log in between.

Merging can't be undone.

## Delete a log

**Delete Log Book** removes the log for good.
